Orders & Delivery

  • How do I order office lunch boxes in Berlin?

    Send us your headcount, delivery address and date at info@migamiga.de or +49 155 65933378. You get a menu proposal and a fixed price back within one working day. Order a one-off box for a meeting, or a weekly lunch plan that repeats automatically for your team.

  • Which Berlin districts do you deliver to?

    We deliver lunch boxes and catering across Berlin, including Mitte, Kreuzberg, Prenzlauer Berg, Friedrichshain, Neukölln, Charlottenburg, Schöneberg and Moabit. Offices outside the Ring are possible too. Send us your postcode and we confirm the delivery fee before you book.

  • How far in advance do I need to order?

    48 hours for one-off lunch box orders, one to two weeks for larger events. Weekly plans are set up once and then repeat automatically, and you can pause or change them with a few days' notice. For short-notice requests, call us and we will check what is still possible.

  • Is there a minimum order for lunch box delivery?

    Yes, five lunch boxes per delivery (Mindestbestellmenge). Smaller teams are still welcome: write to us and we will either combine your order with a nearby delivery or arrange a pick-up, so you are not stuck without lunch.

  • What does delivery cost and when do you deliver?

    Delivery is priced by district and order size, and it is always in the quote before you pay, so nothing new appears on the invoice. We deliver in time for lunch, in a fixed delivery window that you choose when you book.

  • Can we pay by invoice?

    Yes. Companies pay on invoice (auf Rechnung) with VAT and all the details your accounting needs. Private orders can pay by bank transfer, card or cash, and at our pop-ups we take card and cash on the spot.

  • Can I change or cancel an order?

    Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before delivery. After that we have already bought and prepped for you, so cancellations cannot be refunded. Changing the number of boxes or swapping sandwiches is usually fine right up to the same cut-off.

  • Do you deliver to film shoots and production crews?

    Yes. Crew catering is a regular part of what we do: lunch boxes that travel well, labelled per person, delivered to set anywhere in Berlin. Send us your call sheet and we will fit the delivery around your shooting schedule.

Diets & Allergens

  • Do you have vegan and vegetarian sandwiches?

    Yes, vegan and vegetarian sandwiches are a permanent part of the menu rather than an afterthought. You can mix vegan, vegetarian and classic boxes in a single order, and each box is labelled, so nobody has to open three of them to find theirs.

  • Can you handle allergies and intolerances?

    Yes. Tell us when you order and we tell you exactly what is in every sandwich, covering the 14 allergens that EU food labelling law (LMIV) requires. Gluten-free and lactose-free options are available with a little notice.

  • Is the food labelled?

    Every lunch box and platter arrives labelled with its name, ingredients and allergens in English and German. For events we can add printed menu cards for the buffet, so your guests can serve themselves without asking anyone what is in what.

  • Can you cater a fully vegan event?

    Yes, and we do it often for agencies, startups and weddings in Berlin. A fully vegan sandwich and finger-food menu is no more expensive than the standard one. For halal or kosher requirements, ask us and we will tell you plainly what we can and cannot guarantee.

  • Do you bake your own bread?

    Yes. Our bread is kneaded and baked by hand in our own kitchen in Berlin, never bought in from a supplier. That is the whole reason MIGAMIGA exists: a sandwich is only ever as good as the bread it is built on.

  • Where do your ingredients come from?

    We bake our own bread. Every loaf is kneaded and baked in our own Berlin kitchen, then built into sandwiches the same morning they are eaten. The fillings are fresh, straightforward ingredients, bought locally and changed with the season rather than held to one fixed menu.

  • Is your packaging recyclable?

    Yes. Boxes and wrapping are recyclable and we avoid plastic wherever it works. For events at a single location we can deliver on reusable platters and collect them afterwards, which leaves you with almost no waste to clear up.

Events & Volumes

  • What kind of events do you cater in Berlin?

    Corporate catering for team meetings, workshops, conferences, product launches and office parties, plus weddings, birthdays and private celebrations. We work with groups from ten guests up to several hundred, anywhere in Berlin.

  • How many guests can you cater for?

    From ten guests to several hundred. Up to around fifty we can usually work at short notice. Above that we ask for one to two weeks so production and delivery are planned properly rather than improvised on the day.

  • What is included in an events and catering package?

    Freshly made sandwich platters and finger food, delivered ready to serve and set up on site. Plates, napkins, cutlery and serving boards can be added. Menu, delivery time and setup are agreed in advance, so nothing has to be decided on the day.

  • How much does catering in Berlin cost per person?

    The price per person depends on the menu, the guest count and whether you want finger food, full platters or individual lunch boxes. Send us your date, headcount and budget and you get an itemised, fixed quote, usually within one working day and with no obligation.

  • How do I get a catering quote?

    Email info@migamiga.de or call +49 155 65933378 with the date, location, number of guests and any dietary requirements. You get a written proposal with a fixed price, and we hold the date while you decide.

  • Do you cater weddings and private parties?

    Yes. Sandwich and finger-food catering suits relaxed weddings, garden parties and birthdays: easier to eat standing up than a plated dinner and far less formal. Tell us the venue and we work around whatever its kitchen rules allow.

  • Can you deliver to a venue without a kitchen?

    Yes. Everything arrives ready to eat, so a venue with no kitchen, no oven and no staff is not a problem. That makes us a practical choice for coworking spaces, studios, galleries and outdoor locations across Berlin.

Pop-ups & Collaborations

  • What is a MIGAMIGA pop-up?

    A temporary stand where we make and serve our sandwiches fresh on the spot. We set up at markets, office campuses, festivals and partner venues around Berlin, with a short menu built for that particular day.

  • Where is your next pop-up in Berlin?

    Every date and location goes up first on Instagram at @migamiga.de. Spots and times change from week to week, so check there before you head out. New sandwiches and free catering slots are announced in the same place.

  • Can I book a pop-up at my company, campus or event?

    Yes, and it is our favourite kind of booking. Send us the location, the date and the number of people you expect. We bring the stand, the team and the sandwiches; you provide a bit of space and, ideally, power.

  • Do you do collaborations with bars, breweries or galleries?

    Yes. We regularly cook with bars, breweries, coffee roasters and galleries in Berlin for one-night menus and seasonal specials. If you run a space and want food that brings people through the door, write to us with your idea.

  • Do I need to reserve at a pop-up?

    No, just walk up. If you want a larger order for your group, message us the day before and we will have it packed and waiting, so you skip the queue entirely.

  • What can I order at a pop-up and how do I pay?

    A short rotating selection of our sandwiches, always with a vegan option. Card and cash are both accepted. The exact menu for each date goes up on Instagram on the morning of the pop-up.
