Ways we feed Berlin

Sandwich catering in Berlin,
on bread we bake ourselves

MIGAMIGA started with a small complaint: lunch in Berlin is either a sad supermarket triangle or an hour nobody has to spare. So we began making sandwiches the way they ought to be made: bread kneaded and baked by hand in our own kitchen, fillings built that same morning, and enough of them to feed a whole office. What started as a few boxes for friends is now lunch for teams, catering for company events, and a pop-up stand that turns up around the city.

Everything still runs the same way. The bread goes into the oven early, the sandwiches are built on it a few hours later, and we deliver to your door in Mitte, Kreuzberg, Prenzlauer Berg or wherever your office happens to be. You talk to the person who actually makes your order, not a booking form — which is why a quote comes back within one working day, and why we will tell you honestly when something will not work.
